Animate images online with AI motion prompts

Image2Motion AI helps you turn a still image into a video idea in seconds. Upload a photo, illustration, product shot or poster, and the tool analyzes it and writes everything you need to animate it: camera movement prompts, creative scene ideas and a shot-by-shot storyboard. Paste the prompts into an AI image-to-video generator to bring your picture to life.

It is made for content creators, social media managers, marketers, designers, filmmakers and anyone who wants better results from AI video tools without trial and error.

How to use Image2Motion AI

  1. Upload an image. Drop in a photo, artwork or product picture.
  2. Choose your settings. Pick a video style, total duration, aspect ratio and output language, and add an optional idea.
  3. Generate the motion plan. Get four motion prompts, four scene ideas and a complete storyboard.
  4. Create your video. Copy the prompts into your favorite image-to-video tool and render the animation.

Frequently asked questions

What is Image2Motion AI?

Image2Motion AI is a free online tool that studies an image you upload and creates motion prompts, scene ideas and a shot-by-shot video storyboard. You then use those prompts in an AI image-to-video generator to animate your picture.

Does Image2Motion AI generate the video itself?

Image2Motion AI plans the animation. It writes the camera movement and motion prompts and a storyboard that you paste into a video generator such as Runway, Kling or Pika to render the final clip.

Is Image2Motion AI free to use?

Yes, to start. Every new account gets 25 free tokens, and one token creates one complete motion plan. When you need more, you can upgrade to the Pro plan.

What kind of images work best?

Clear, well-lit images with a visible subject work best: portraits, landscapes, products, food, architecture, illustrations and posters. Very low resolution or heavily cluttered images give less precise results.

Which languages are supported?

You can choose from 19 output languages, including English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Turkish, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Generation prompts can stay in English, which most video tools understand best.

Are my uploaded images stored?

Your image is resized in your browser and sent only to generate the plan. This site does not keep a library of uploaded images.

Billing FAQ

What is a token?

One token is used each time you generate a motion plan from an image. Failed generations do not use a token.

What happens when my free tokens run out?

You can upgrade to Pro at any time to keep generating. Your account and past results stay available.

Do unused tokens expire?

No. Unused tokens stay in your account.

How do I cancel?

Open this page while signed in and click Manage billing. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Which payment methods can I use?

The secure checkout shows the payment methods available for your country. Prices are shown in USD and taxes are handled at checkout.
